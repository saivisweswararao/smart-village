Register your private admin alert device

Register this phone to receive private alerts when a new Penubaka complaint is submitted or a blood request is received. Only the single registered device receives these alerts. Public announcement subscriptions are not affected.

Notification: Complaint: “A new complaint has been submitted.”
Blood request: requester name, full mobile number and required blood group.

When tapped: Opens the relevant community request page. You may need to sign in.
