Rejected payment adjustments

Rejected transactions are excluded from expenditure, assuming they were included in the gross payment totals and were not subsequently paid.

How to read these figures

Receipts are amounts recorded as received in Panchayat accounts. Payments are gross payment entries. Rejected payments shown above are subtracted to calculate adjusted payments. The closing balance is an accounting calculation based on the figures supplied; it is not a verified bank balance. The 2026–27 figures are year-to-date.

Data source: figures supplied for this community dashboard based on eGramSwaraj statements. This is a community information page and is not an official Panchayat website or an audited financial statement.
