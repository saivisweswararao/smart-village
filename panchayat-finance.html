Failed payments are removed from the spending total, assuming they were included in the original total and were not paid later.

How to read these figures

Money received means amounts recorded as received in Panchayat accounts. Money spent is the total after removing failed payments. The remaining balance is calculated from the figures supplied; it is not a verified bank balance. The 2026–27 figures are year-to-date.

Data source: figures supplied for this community dashboard based on eGramSwaraj statements. This is a community information page and is not an official Panchayat website or an audited financial statement.
